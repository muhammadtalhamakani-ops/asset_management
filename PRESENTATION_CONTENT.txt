FIELDSTOCK - CONSTRUCTION ASSET MANAGEMENT PORTAL
 CONTENT (SLIDE-BY-SLIDE)
====================================================


SLIDE 1 - PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------
FieldStock - Construction Asset Management Portal

- Full-stack web app: React + TypeScript (Vite) frontend, Node.js/Express + MongoDB backend
- Company profile used for demo data: Al Marasi Construction LLC, Dubai
- 6 active project sites:
  1. Dubai Creek Harbour - Tower C
  2. Business Bay Business Park Extension
  3. Dubai Marina - Tower 3
  4. Al Quoz Logistics Hub
  5. Palm Jumeirah Villa Development
  6. Jebel Ali Industrial Yard
- Tracks the full asset lifecycle: register -> receive -> issue -> transfer -> inspect -> return/damage -> audit


SLIDE 2 - LOGIN
----------------------------------------------------
- Secure sign-in screen with email/password
- "Keep me signed in" + forgot password option
- Branded split-screen layout showing live site stats (tracked items, active locations, audit completion)
- 5 seeded role-based accounts:
  1. Ahmed Al Mansouri - Site Manager
  2. Fatima Al Rashid - Admin
  3. Ravi Kumar - Store Keeper
  4. Sara Abdullah - Safety Officer
  5. John D'Souza - Site Engineer


SLIDE 3 - DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------------------
- KPI cards: total asset value, available stock, issued/in-use, items needing attention
- Live data: 19 assets, total asset value approx. AED 3,942,540
- Recent movement activity feed (issues, transfers, receipts, inspections)
- Stock alerts panel (low stock, inspection due, damaged items)
- "Assets by location" bar chart across active sites
- Quick action buttons: Receive delivery / Issue asset / Transfer asset


SLIDE 4 - ASSET REGISTER (ASSET MASTER)
----------------------------------------------------
- Full 22-field master record per asset:
  ID, name, type, serial, category, sub-category, description, manufacturer, model,
  unit, quantity, minimum stock, unit cost, total value, supplier, purchase date,
  project, location, status, QR code, attachments, remarks
- Search + filter by location/type/status, paginated table
- Inline edit/delete per record
- Sample categories:
  - Machinery: excavators, tower cranes, concrete mixers, generators
  - Steel: rebar 16mm/12mm, steel mesh sheets
  - Materials: cement, plywood formwork, ceramic tiles, curing compound
  - Hardware: safety helmets, harnesses, power tools, cable reels, fire extinguishers
  - Scaffolding and Formwork systems


SLIDE 5 - INVENTORY
----------------------------------------------------
- Stock view across site locations
- Opening / received / issued / available stock breakdown
- Example: Rebar 16mm - 1,200 kg at Steel Yard (minimum stock 300 kg)
- Highlights low-stock items (e.g. Ceramic Floor Tiles flagged "Low stock")


SLIDE 6 - RECEIVE ASSETS (GRN & DELIVERIES)
----------------------------------------------------
- Record supplier deliveries -> automatically updates inventory
- GRN-numbered records with supplier, destination, quantity, date, status
- Live data: 22 total movement records, including receives such as:
  - Conares Steel -> rebar and mesh deliveries
  - Al Jaber Building Materials -> cement, PPE restock
  - RAK Ceramics Trading -> floor tile shipment
  - Dubai Cable Company -> cable reel stock


SLIDE 7 - ISSUE & ALLOCATE
----------------------------------------------------
- Allocate assets to responsible teams/individuals with accountability
- Search/filter by status (Issued / Pending)
- Example real records:
  - Excavator CAT 320 -> Earthworks Team (Ahmed Al Mansouri)
  - Safety Helmets (40 units) -> new inductee batch
  - Rebar 16mm (300 kg) -> Steel Fixing Team
  - Power Drill Sets (24) -> MEP Team Zone A


SLIDE 8 - TRANSFERS
----------------------------------------------------
- Move assets between warehouses, sites, and zones
- From/To location, quantity, project, status tracking
- Example real records:
  - Tower Crane transferred from Al Quoz Logistics Hub -> Dubai Marina Tower 3 (erected on site)
  - Scaffold Frame Set relocated to Dubai Creek Harbour
  - Passenger Hoist mobilised to Dubai Marina Tower 3


SLIDE 9 - ASSEMBLIES
----------------------------------------------------
- Parent-assembly and component (kit) tracking with condition status
- Live data: 3 assemblies
  1. Facade Scaffold Assembly (DCH Tower C)
     - 120/120 frames, 38/40 guard rail posts (Missing) - status: In use
  2. Core Wall Formwork Set (Business Bay)
     - 96/100 tie rods (Damaged) - status: Available
  3. Access Stair Tower (Palm Jumeirah)
     - 6/8 stair flights (Missing), 14/16 handrails (Damaged) - status: Incomplete
- Component-level quantity stepper + condition toggle (Pass/Damaged/Missing), persisted to database
- Create-assembly workflow with dynamic component rows
- View assembly QR and delete assembly actions


SLIDE 10 - QR SCANNER
----------------------------------------------------
- Scan or look up assets by ID or QR code
- Instantly shows current location, assigned party, inspection status
- Example QR codes: QR-MAC-021, QR-SCF-031, QR-ASM-011


SLIDE 11 - INSPECTIONS
----------------------------------------------------
- Safety and condition checklists for reusable assets and assemblies
- Live data: 11 inspections across excavators, tower cranes, mixers, generators,
  formwork, PPE, fire extinguishers, cable reels
- Outcomes: mix of Pass / Needs Repair, for example:
  - Tower Crane - hydraulic leak identified
  - Diesel Generator - fuel gauge sensor fault
  - Fire Extinguishers - two units below gauge pressure
- Corrective action assignment workflow with due dates
- Fields: last inspection date, inspector name, checklist items, condition (Good/Fair/Poor)


SLIDE 12 - RETURNS & DAMAGE
----------------------------------------------------
- Records returns, damage, and loss with responsibility and resolution tracking
- Live data: 5 return/damage/loss records
  1. Safety Harnesses - 2 damaged (torn stitching), replacement pending
  2. Power Drill Sets - full clean return
  3. Plywood Formwork - 3 damaged + 2 missing, under investigation
  4. Cable Reel - 1 damaged (jacket cut), repair pending
  5. Curing Compound - 3 drums missing, under investigation
- Resolution states: Closed / Replacement pending / Repair pending / Under investigation


SLIDE 13 - REPORTS & AUDIT
----------------------------------------------------
- Exportable CSV audit trail of every movement
- Aggregate metrics: current stock, total asset value, open variance count, audit event count
- Full chronological history: who recorded each action, from/to location, timestamps


SLIDE 14 - ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
----------------------------------------------------
- Frontend: React 18 + TypeScript, Vite, custom CSS design system
- Backend: Node.js/Express REST API, MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
- Database collections: assets, movements, inspections, returns, assemblies, users
- All screens are fully wired to live MongoDB data - no mock/static data remaining
